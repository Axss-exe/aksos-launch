# AKSOS DESIGN DOCTRINE

## 01 — Purpose
AKSOS is not presented as another SaaS product. The website is an entry point into an intelligence infrastructure. The visitor should feel they are entering a system that helps them see relationships, understand context, identify opportunity, and act.

## 02 — Central relationship
Visitor = HERO.
AKSOS = ALLY / infrastructure.
ATIS = intelligence/perspective.
RITA = investigation/relationships.
Batana = human/action.

Preferred mental model:
YOU ↔ AKSOS ↔ INTELLIGENCE ↔ OPPORTUNITY ↔ ACTION

## 03 — Experience arc
SEE → CONNECT → UNDERSTAND → ACT → BUILD

SEE: fragmented signals.
CONNECT: relationships become visible.
UNDERSTAND: relationships require context and perspective.
ACT: understanding enables decisions and opportunities.
BUILD: participation strengthens the network.

## 04 — Core transformation
SIGNAL → RELATIONSHIP → CONTEXT → UNDERSTANDING → OPPORTUNITY → ACTION

This grammar should appear in diagrams, motion, transitions, copy, architecture, and CTA logic.

## 05 — Character
Quiet confidence; editorial restraint; technical precision; human agency; African context. Technical but not sterile. Premium but not ornamental. African without clichés.

## 06 — Reference principles
Learn principles from premium product experiences such as Apple, Palantir, OpenAI, and the provided Optimus reference:
- Apple: spatial storytelling, controlled reveals, hierarchy, pacing.
- Palantir: systems thinking, relationship visualization, operational language.
- OpenAI: editorial restraint and conceptual clarity.
- Optimus: information choreography, changing states, interactive product storytelling.
Never copy exact layouts, artwork, logos, or proprietary graphics.

## 07 — Color
Use the existing warm paper / ink / rust / green system. Color primarily communicates state, priority, activation, category, and transition. Avoid decorative gradients and neon accents.

## 08 — Typography
SERIF: conceptual, strategic, emotional statements.
SANS: navigation, explanation, interface.
MONO: system labels, metadata, technical annotations, states.
Do not make every heading enormous. Control line length and hierarchy.

## 09 — Composition
A section is a composition with a focal point, supporting information, negative space, reading order, interaction state, and transition. Do not force every section into one template.

## 10 — Rhythm
Alternate density, quiet, movement, stillness, statement, system, human, and infrastructure. Whitespace should create hierarchy or anticipation—not imitate premium websites.

## 11 — Diagram doctrine
Before coding ask:
1. What question does this diagram answer?
2. What are its entities?
3. What relationships exist?
4. What is primary?
5. What changes over time?
6. What must remain visible?
7. What can be simplified on mobile?

Every node and line has semantic meaning. Suggested connection language: solid = direct; lighter/dashed = contextual; active = relevant; animated = signal/state transition.

## 12 — Motion doctrine
Motion is explanatory. Preferred progression:
signal enters → relationship activates → context appears → interpretation emerges → consequence appears.

Avoid constant floating, excessive parallax, repeated fade-ups, large springy scaling, and gratuitous rotation.

## 13 — Interaction doctrine
Interaction should reveal information. Hover can emphasize relationships or metadata; focus must provide equivalent access; mobile replaces hover with tap/selection. Never destroy system context when an item is selected.

## 14 — Section storytelling
Problem: What is wrong with information as it exists?
Ally: Who helps me navigate it?
What We Build: What is the system?
ATIS: How does intelligence emerge?
RITA: How do relationships become stories?
Batana: How does intelligence become action?
Network: What happens when the system compounds?

## 15 — ATIS
ATIS is the intelligence/perspective layer:
COMPANY + POLICY + CAPITAL + PROJECT + RELATIONSHIP → ATIS → WHAT DOES THIS MEAN FOR YOU?

## 16 — RITA
RITA is the relationship/investigation layer:
SOURCE → EVENT → ENTITY → RELATIONSHIP → CONTEXT → STORY

## 17 — Batana
Batana is the human/action layer:
PERSON / ORGANIZATION → OBJECTIVE → UNDERSTANDING → INTELLIGENCE → OPPORTUNITY → ACTION

The homepage leads into the existing Batana route without recreating the application.

## 18 — African context
Communicate Africa through people, enterprises, institutions, capital, projects, policy, markets, and cross-border relationships. Do not reduce Africa to maps, flags, safari imagery, or generic emerging-market clichés.

## 19 — Responsive doctrine
Responsive design preserves meaning, not geometry. Mobile may remove secondary nodes, change horizontal flows to vertical, and simplify relationships.

## 20 — Accessibility
Every interaction has keyboard access, focus-visible states, readable contrast, semantic labels, and reduced-motion behavior. Animation is never the only information carrier.

## 21 — Performance
Prefer CSS, SVG, Framer Motion, and deterministic React. Avoid large visualization libraries for simple diagrams and unnecessary animation loops.

## 22 — Quality bar
The page should feel minimal, intelligent, editorial, technical, alive, human, African, and systems-oriented.

Desired reaction:
“I am entering an intelligence system.”

Not:
“This is a redesigned marketing website.”

## 23 — Anti-patterns
Reject generic SaaS, card grids, dashboard cosplay, AI-glow aesthetics, decorative networks, animation showcases, content walls, oversized typography everywhere, empty whitespace, and mobile-shrunk desktop.

## 24 — Design success
A visitor should be able to explain:
“AKSOS helps operators see relationships and context that are difficult to see alone. ATIS helps interpret intelligence, RITA helps investigate relationships, and Batana helps turn understanding into action.”
