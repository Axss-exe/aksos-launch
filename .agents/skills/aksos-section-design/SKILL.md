# AKSOS Section Design
A section is a composition with a purpose.

## Section brief
Before coding a major section define: purpose, primary message, visitor question, focal point, supporting content, interaction, transition, responsive composition.

## Composition vocabulary
Statement-first.
Diagram-first.
Asymmetric editorial.
Full-width system scene.
Operator-centered.
Transitional.

These are patterns, not templates to repeat.

## Rhythm
Vary density, visual complexity, typography, diagram scale, and reading direction.

## Visual-first rule
If a diagram communicates the idea better than prose, make the diagram primary.

## Transition
The end of a section should create a reason to continue through a state change, handoff, short statement, or emerging relationship.

## Quality
The visitor should quickly know what the section means, what changed, why the visual exists, and why the next section matters.
